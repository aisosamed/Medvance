Questions you may have

Is this safe?

Treatment is only recommended after a doctor reviews your health history. Some people aren't suitable, for example during pregnancy or with certain medical conditions, and we'll tell you honestly if it isn't right for you.

Will I lose the same amount as your client?

Everyone responds differently. Her result is her own, and your doctor will set realistic expectations for you.

Do I need to be a certain size to qualify?

Your doctor decides based on your health, BMI and history. The consultation is the way to find out.

What is your pricing?

Prices vary by medicine and dose. View our current GLP-1 product prices; your doctor will review suitability and confirm your treatment plan.

What happens if I stop treatment?

Weight can return after treatment stops, so we plan for the long term with you, including healthy habits that help you keep your results.

How does delivery work?

Your treatment is packed cold and delivered anywhere in Nigeria after your consultation.
