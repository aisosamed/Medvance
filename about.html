THE FOUNDER

Meet Aisosa Omoriyekemwen

MedVanceNg was founded by Aisosa Omoriyekemwen, a licensed pharmacist with over six years of experience across clinical pharmacy, rehabilitation care, pharmaceutical operations, and health technology.

Her career was shaped by leading medication management, therapeutic optimization, pharmacovigilance, and quality assurance across multidisciplinary healthcare settings, where patient safety and evidence-based care remained at the center of every decision.

She developed expertise in medication therapy management, clinical governance, and pharmaceutical service delivery, while contributing to healthcare initiatives that improved medication adherence and promoted responsible medicine use for more than 1,000 individuals in underserved communities.

MedVanceNg was established to bring that same standard of clinical excellence beyond traditional healthcare settings, creating a trusted platform where Nigerians could access professional pharmaceutical guidance, medically guided care, and a patient first experience built on safety, integrity, and clinical expertise.
