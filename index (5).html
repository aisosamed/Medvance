FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

GLP-1 & Tirzepatide in Nigeria

Answers to common questions about buying Mounjaro, Ozempic and tirzepatide from a licensed Nigerian pharmacy.

Is Mounjaro or Ozempic legally available in Nigeria?

Yes. Both are legally dispensed as prescription-only medicines through NAFDAC-registered pharmacies like MedVanceNg, after a licensed prescriber reviews your health information.

What is compounded tirzepatide, and how is it different from Mounjaro?

Compounded tirzepatide is prepared by a licensed compounding pharmacy rather than the original manufacturer. It is a distinct preparation from branded Mounjaro, dispensed only under prescriber supervision, and your prescriber will discuss whether it's an appropriate option for you.

Do I need a prescription to buy GLP-1 medicines?

Yes, always. GLP-1 medicines are prescription-only. Selecting a product on this site starts a review with our licensed prescriber — it does not complete a direct, unchecked purchase.

What tirzepatide doses do you carry?

Compounded tirzepatide is available in 2.5mg, 5mg and 10mg strengths, and branded Mounjaro is available across all approved strengths. Your prescriber determines your starting dose and any titration.

How is Mounjaro or Ozempic delivered in Nigeria?

Once your prescriber confirms your order, we arrange discreet, temperature-appropriate delivery nationwide. Delivery timelines depend on your location.
