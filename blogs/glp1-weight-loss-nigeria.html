Complete Guide to GLP-1 Medications in Nigeria

Written by Dr. Aisosa, Founder, MedVanceNg · Last updated: September 2026
What is this guide about?

This guide explains what GLP-1 medications are, how they work, which ones are available in Nigeria, who they may be suitable for, who should avoid them, and how medically guided access works through a NAFDAC registered pharmacy like MedVanceNg.

What Are GLP-1 Medications?

GLP-1 medications are a class of prescription medicines that mimic a naturally occurring gut hormone called glucagon-like peptide-1. This hormone plays a role in regulating blood sugar and appetite. Medicines in this class were originally developed to treat type 2 diabetes, and several — including tirzepatide (sold under the brand name Mounjaro) and semaglutide (sold under the brand name Ozempic) — are now also used for medical weight management under prescriber supervision.

These medicines are not lifestyle or over-the-counter products. They are prescription-only medicines, meaning a licensed prescriber must review a person's health information before any medicine is dispensed.

How GLP-1 Medications Work for Weight Management

GLP-1 medications slow gastric emptying and act on appetite centres in the brain, which typically leads to reduced hunger and earlier feelings of fullness. Over time, and combined with dietary and lifestyle changes, this can support meaningful weight management for appropriate candidates. Individual results vary, and these medicines work best as part of a broader, medically supervised plan rather than as a stand-alone solution.

GLP-1 Medications Available in Nigeria

Through MedVanceNg, three types of GLP-1 medicines are currently available, each dispensed only after prescriber review:

  • Mounjaro (tirzepatide) — the original manufacturer product, available across all approved strengths.
  • Ozempic (semaglutide) — the original manufacturer product, available across all approved strengths.
  • Compounded tirzepatide — the same active molecule prepared by a licensed compounding pharmacy rather than the original manufacturer, available in 2.5mg, 5mg, and 10mg strengths.

Compounded tirzepatide is a distinct preparation from branded Mounjaro. A prescriber will discuss whether it is an appropriate option based on individual circumstances.

Who May Consider GLP-1 Medications

GLP-1 medications are generally considered for adults seeking medical support for weight management, particularly where lifestyle changes alone have not achieved the desired outcome. Suitability is always determined on a case-by-case basis by a licensed prescriber, based on a full review of medical history.

Who Should Not Use GLP-1 Medicines

GLP-1 medicines are not suitable for everyone. A prescriber will screen for contraindications before dispensing, including:

  • A personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2)
  • A history of pancreatitis
  • Pregnancy or plans to become pregnant

This list is not exhaustive. Always disclose your full medical history during prescriber review — this is not a substitute for individualized medical advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are GLP-1 medications?

GLP-1 medications, including tirzepatide (Mounjaro) and semaglutide (Ozempic), are prescription medicines that mimic a natural gut hormone affecting appetite and blood sugar regulation. They are used for type 2 diabetes and, under prescriber supervision, medical weight management.

Are GLP-1 medications available in Nigeria?

Yes. Mounjaro, Ozempic, and compounded tirzepatide can be legally dispensed in Nigeria through a NAFDAC registered pharmacy, as prescription-only medicines, after review by a licensed prescriber.

Can anyone use GLP-1 medications for weight loss?

No. These medicines require individualized assessment by a licensed prescriber, who screens for contraindications such as a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2, pancreatitis, and pregnancy, before dispensing.

What is the difference between Mounjaro and compounded tirzepatide?

Mounjaro is the branded, manufacturer-produced version of tirzepatide. Compounded tirzepatide is the same active molecule prepared by a licensed compounding pharmacy. Both are dispensed only after prescriber review.
